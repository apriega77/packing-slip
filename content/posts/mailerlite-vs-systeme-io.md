Small shop owners who want a free starting plan often end up looking at two very different tools: [MailerLite](aff:mailerlite) and [Systeme.io](aff:systeme). Both offer a free plan with no time limit, yet their limits push a growing shop in opposite directions.

## Free plans side by side

| | MailerLite Free | Systeme.io Free |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly price | $0 | $0 |
| Contact limit | 250 subscribers | 2,000 contacts |
| Monthly email sends | 2,500 emails | Unlimited |
| Automation limit | 3 active automations (5 steps each) | 1 automation rule, 1 workflow |
| Forms, campaigns, and tags | 3 active forms | 1 email campaign, 1 tag |
| Websites and landing pages | 1 website and 1 landing page | 1 website, 3 sales funnels (15 steps) |
| Custom domain | Not included | 1 custom domain |
| Tool branding | MailerLite logo stays | Not stated on pricing table |
| Support | Knowledge base and webinars | 24/7 email support |

We read these figures from each company's public pricing page on 14 November 2026.

## The subscriber wall vs the feature wall

The biggest difference on paper is list size. MailerLite Free tops out at 250 subscribers and 2,500 emails a month. MailerLite's pricing FAQ states that if your subscriber count goes over 250 on the Free plan, it holds off sending further emails until you upgrade or reduce your subscriber count.

Systeme.io Free allows up to 2,000 contacts and unlimited emails sent, with unlimited email newsletters and no credit card required. On raw list room, Systeme.io lets a small shop stay at $0 for eight times as many contacts.

The catch with Systeme.io Free is not list size. It is organization and automation. The Free plan allows 1 email campaign, 1 automation rule, 1 workflow, and 1 tag. You can send unlimited one-off newsletters and run one basic automated sequence, such as a welcome email with a shop coupon, but you cannot split subscribers across multiple tags or run separate automated sequences for different product types until you pay.

MailerLite Free gives you slightly more room to organize early automations (3 active automations of up to 5 steps each, plus 3 active forms), though multi-trigger automations are reserved for the Power plan, not Free or Comfort.

## Pricing at 500 and 1,000 subscribers

Once a shop outgrows a free tier, the two pricing models behave differently. MailerLite's paid plans (Comfort and Power) step up by subscriber tier, while Systeme.io's first paid plan (Startup) has one flat price up to 5,000 contacts.

| List size | MailerLite Comfort | MailerLite Power | Systeme.io |
|---|---|---|---|
| 500 subscribers (monthly) | $12.00 / month | $25.00 / month | $0 on Free (up to 2,000) |
| 500 subscribers (yearly) | $10.80 / month ($129.60/yr) | $22.50 / month ($270.00/yr) | $0 on Free (up to 2,000) |
| 1,000 subscribers (monthly) | $19.00 / month | $39.00 / month | $0 on Free, or $17/mo Startup |
| 1,000 subscribers (yearly) | $17.10 / month ($205.20/yr) | $35.10 / month ($421.20/yr) | $0 on Free, or $170/yr Startup |

Monthly sending caps also differ across MailerLite's two paid tiers. Comfort sets your monthly email allowance at 10 times the top of your subscriber tier: 5,000 emails a month at 500 subscribers and 10,000 emails a month at 1,000 subscribers. Power removes that monthly email cap, subject to MailerLite's fair use policy. Systeme.io keeps emails sent unlimited on both Free and Startup.

At 1,000 subscribers, MailerLite Comfort costs $19 a month on monthly billing (or $17.10 a month billed yearly). At that same list size, Systeme.io is still $0 if 1 automation rule, 1 workflow, and 1 tag are enough for your shop, or $17 a month ($170 a year) on Startup for up to 5,000 contacts, 10 email campaigns, 10 automation rules, 5 workflows, and 10 tags.

## Branding, domains, and extra tools

If you want your signup page on your own domain from day one, Systeme.io Free includes 1 custom domain (and 3 on Startup). MailerLite keeps custom domains and logo removal off the Free plan; both are included starting on Comfort (along with 10 active forms, 10 websites and landing pages, and 50 active automations). MailerLite Free includes email and form A/B testing, while auto resend campaigns and landing page split testing require Comfort or higher. Systeme.io Free includes 1 A/B test and 1 coupon code.

The trade-off is clutter. MailerLite is built around email newsletters, signup forms, landing pages, and email automations. Systeme.io's pricing page lists 1 blog, 1 course, 1 community, CRM pipelines, booking calendars, and physical product order bumps alongside email tools. If you only sell on Etsy and want a clean newsletter editor without extra menus, MailerLite has far fewer unrelated features in the way.

## Which one fits your shop

- Choose [Systeme.io](aff:systeme) if keeping your monthly cost at $0 past 250 contacts matters most, and your shop only needs a signup form, one welcome sequence, and regular newsletters.
- Choose [MailerLite](aff:mailerlite) if you want an email-focused tool from the start, expect to stay under 250 subscribers at first, and do not mind paying from $12 a month (Comfort) once your list grows or once you want custom domains and logo removal.
