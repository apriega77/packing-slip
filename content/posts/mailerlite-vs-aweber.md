Once an online shop expects to outgrow free starter limits, [MailerLite](aff:mailerlite) and [AWeber](aff:aweber) charge in similar steps. Both price their paid plans by subscriber tier, and both step up at 500, 1,000, and 2,500 subscribers. Where they differ is how much each entry paid tier includes before pushing you up to a higher plan.

## Pricing at 500, 1,000, and 2,500 subscribers

MailerLite sells two self-serve paid tiers (Comfort and Power), with a 10% discount on yearly billing. AWeber sells two main self-serve tiers (Lite and Plus), plus a Done For You tier at the same monthly or annual subscription price as Plus with an added setup fee (`$79` promotional, crossed out from `$599`).

| Tier and billing | MailerLite Comfort | MailerLite Power | AWeber Lite | AWeber Plus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 500 subscribers (monthly) | $12.00 / mo | $25.00 / mo | $15.00 / mo | $30.00 / mo |
| 500 subscribers (yearly) | $10.80 / mo ($129.60/yr) | $22.50 / mo ($270.00/yr) | $12.50 / mo ($149.99/yr) | $20.00 / mo ($239.99/yr) |
| 1,000 subscribers (monthly) | $19.00 / mo | $39.00 / mo | $24.00 / mo | $44.00 / mo |
| 1,000 subscribers (yearly) | $17.10 / mo ($205.20/yr) | $35.10 / mo ($421.20/yr) | $20.00 / mo ($239.99/yr) | $30.00 / mo ($359.99/yr) |
| 2,500 subscribers (monthly) | $33.00 / mo | $49.00 / mo | $45.00 / mo | $95.00 / mo |
| 2,500 subscribers (yearly) | $29.70 / mo ($356.40/yr) | $44.10 / mo ($529.20/yr) | $37.50 / mo ($449.99/yr) | $65.00 / mo ($779.99/yr) |

We read these US dollar prices from each company's live pricing calculator on 14 November 2026. On AWeber's annual toggle, the card headings show rounded monthly equivalents (for example, `$12.50/month` for `$149.99/year`, and `$37.50/month` for `$449.99/year`).

On entry paid plans, MailerLite Comfort costs less than AWeber Lite at every step: $3 less per month at 500 subscribers, $5 less at 1,000, and $12 less at 2,500 on monthly billing. Yearly billing keeps MailerLite Comfort ahead at all three tiers ($129.60 vs $149.99 at 500; $205.20 vs $239.99 at 1,000; $356.40 vs $449.99 at 2,500).

At the higher tiers, the gap changes with billing cycle. At 500 subscribers paid yearly, AWeber Plus ($239.99/year) is cheaper than MailerLite Power ($270.00/year). By 1,000 and 2,500 subscribers, MailerLite Power costs less than AWeber Plus on both monthly and yearly billing, and by 2,500 subscribers MailerLite Power ($49/month monthly) is close to AWeber Lite ($45/month monthly).

## Entry paid plans: Comfort vs Lite

For most small shops, the real comparison is MailerLite Comfort against AWeber Lite, because those are the two entry paid plans.

| Feature | MailerLite Comfort | AWeber Lite |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly email sends | 10x subscriber tier ceiling | 10x subscriber volume |
| Remove tool branding | Included | Not included (Plus only) |
| Custom domains | Included | Included |
| Websites and landing pages | 10 active websites and landing pages | 3 landing pages |
| Signup forms | 10 active forms | Sign up forms included |
| Automations | 50 active automations (100 steps) | 3 email automations |
| Split / A/B testing | Email, form, landing page, multivariate (up to 8) | Not included (Plus only) |
| Lists and segmentation | Dynamic email content, custom unsubscribe builder | 1 list profile, 1 saved custom segment |
| Auto resend to non-openers | Included | Not included |
| Support | Email support | Phone, email, and live chat |

Both entry plans cap monthly sends at 10 times your subscriber tier (5,000 emails at 500 subscribers, 10,000 at 1,000, and 25,000 at 2,500). That leaves room for roughly two campaigns a week to a full list, plus automated welcome emails.

The sharper split is in what stays locked on AWeber Lite. AWeber Lite keeps AWeber branding on emails and landing pages, caps you at 3 email automations, 3 landing pages, 1 list profile, and 1 saved custom segment, and leaves out email split testing and behavioral automation. MailerLite Comfort includes logo removal, 50 automations, 10 websites and landing pages, landing page and multivariate testing, dynamic email content, and auto resend to non-openers at its entry paid price.

Where AWeber Lite wins is direct human support. AWeber Lite includes phone, email, and live chat support from day one, while MailerLite Comfort limits you to email support until you either reach 50,000 subscribers or move up to Power.

## Stepping up to Power or Plus

If your shop outgrows the entry paid tier, the upgrade paths solve different bottlenecks:

- **MailerLite Power** removes the 10x monthly sending cap (moving to unlimited monthly sends under MailerLite's fair use policy), lifts websites, landing pages, forms, automations, and team seats to unlimited, adds multiple triggers per automation (up to 3 triggers on a single workflow), and includes 24/7 live chat plus priority email support.
- **AWeber Plus** raises monthly email sends from 10x to 12x subscriber volume, lifts list profiles, automations, landing pages, and custom segments to unlimited, removes AWeber branding, adds behavioral automation and email split testing, includes free migration, and upgrades support to priority phone, chat, and email. AWeber Plus also lowers transaction fees on AWeber's own checkout tools from 1.0% on Lite to 0.6%, though sellers running checkout through Etsy do not pay AWeber transaction fees.

## What about starting free first?

If you are comparing these two before your list reaches 500 people, AWeber's separate free plan page ([aweber.com/free.htm](https://www.aweber.com/free.htm)) covers up to 500 subscribers and 3,000 emails a month with no time limit, no credit card, and 24/7 phone, email, and chat support, though its feature-by-feature limits are not tabulated alongside paid plans on the main pricing table. MailerLite Free caps at 250 subscribers and 2,500 emails a month, holds off sending if you pass 250 subscribers, and limits support to its knowledge base and webinars.

## Which one fits your shop

- Choose [MailerLite](aff:mailerlite) if you want lower monthly and annual costs from 500 to 2,500 subscribers, logo removal on your entry paid plan, and room for more than 3 automations and landing pages without upgrading to a higher tier.
- Choose [AWeber](aff:aweber) if phone and live chat support at lower list sizes matters more to your shop than removing branding or running split tests, or if you want a 500-subscriber free runway before you start paying.
