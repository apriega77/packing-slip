A new Etsy shop usually starts with zero subscribers and wants a way to collect signups without paying a monthly fee yet. Three tools show up most often for small sellers: [Systeme.io](aff:systeme), [MailerLite](aff:mailerlite), and [AWeber](aff:aweber). Each puts its free ceiling in a different place, and AWeber separates its ongoing free plan from the 14-day trial shown on its main pricing page.

## The free options side by side

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Time limit | No expiry | No expiry (14-day trial of paid features) | No time limit | 14 days |
| Credit card at signup | No | No | No | Not stated on pricing page |

| Support | 24/7 email | Knowledge base and webinars | 24/7 phone, email, chat | Phone, email, chat (priority on Plus) |

We checked these terms directly on each company's pricing and free-plan pages on 14 November 2026.

## Systeme.io Free: most room for contacts, tightest list organization

[Systeme.io](aff:systeme) gives a new shop the largest free subscriber allowance: up to 2,000 contacts and unlimited emails sent, with no credit card required and no expiry date. It also includes 1 custom domain, 1 A/B test, 1 coupon code, and 0% transaction fees.

The limit you hit first is list organization. Systeme.io Free restricts you to 1 email campaign, 1 automation rule, 1 workflow, and 1 tag. You can collect signups on a landing page, trigger one automated welcome email, and send unlimited one-off newsletters, but you cannot tag buyers by product type or run separate automated sequences until you upgrade to Startup ($17/month monthly or $170/year annual, up to 5,000 contacts). Its dashboard also includes funnels, a blog, a course, and a community builder that an Etsy seller may never use.

## MailerLite Free: focused newsletter editor, smallest contact cap

[MailerLite](aff:mailerlite) keeps its Free tier focused on email. With no credit card required, you get up to 250 subscribers, 2,500 monthly emails, 2 user seats, 3 active signup forms, 1 website, 1 landing page, and 3 active automations (up to 5 steps each, single trigger per automation), plus form and email A/B testing.

New MailerLite accounts also get a 14-day trial of premium features with no credit card required. According to MailerLite's pricing FAQ, when the 14 days end, those premium features stop until you upgrade, while your subscribers, automations, forms, and sites stay in your account.

The strict limit is the 250-subscriber ceiling. MailerLite's FAQ states that once active subscribers pass 250 on Free, sending locks for both campaigns and automations until you upgrade or remove subscribers. Custom domains and removing the MailerLite logo also wait until Comfort, which starts at $12/month monthly ($10.80/month or $129.60/year billed yearly) for up to 500 subscribers. Support on Free is limited to the knowledge base and webinars.

## AWeber Free vs AWeber's 14-day trial

AWeber shows two different $0 entry paths depending on which page you open:

- **AWeber's 14-day trial (`aweber.com/pricing.htm`)**: The main pricing table leads with "Try AWeber free with a 14-day trial" across its Lite, Plus, and Done For You plans. Those buttons link directly to `/checkout.htm` with the 14-day trial parameter. The pricing page itself does not state whether a card is required at checkout or what happens when day 14 ends, only showing the ongoing paid prices beneath each card (starting with Lite at $15/month monthly or $149.99/year for up to 500 subscribers, and Plus at $30/month monthly or $239.99/year).
- **AWeber Free (`aweber.com/free.htm` and `aweber.com/free-email-marketing.htm`)**: Linked from the pricing page header and its "Get started for free" section, AWeber's dedicated free plan requires no credit card and states no time limit. It covers up to 500 subscribers and 3,000 emails per month, and lists all email design tools (including smart designer), campaign automations, sign up forms, landing pages, integrations, and 24/7 phone, email, and chat support. Unlike its paid comparison table, AWeber's free pages do not list exact caps on landing pages, automation counts, or whether AWeber branding stays on emails.

If your goal is to stay at $0 while starting your shop list, make sure you sign up through AWeber's Free plan page rather than starting a 14-day paid-tier trial from the pricing table unless you already plan to move to Lite or Plus after two weeks.

## Check Etsy's consent rule before collecting emails

Before you send your first campaign on any free plan, keep Etsy's seller rules in mind. Etsy's published guidance for sellers requires a buyer's express consent before sending marketing emails, even when Etsy gave you their email address to fulfil an order. Do not paste past Etsy order emails into your new list. Instead, share a signup form link on a physical packing slip, in your shop announcement, or in your shop about section so buyers opt in themselves, and leave the unsubscribe link active in every email.

## Which free plan fits a new Etsy shop

- **Pick [Systeme.io](aff:systeme) Free** if you want the longest $0 runway (up to 2,000 contacts), want a custom domain from day one, and only need one welcome email plus regular newsletters.
- **Pick [MailerLite](aff:mailerlite) Free** if you want a clean email-and-form workspace without extra funnel or course menus, and you do not mind upgrading from $12/month once your list passes 250 subscribers.
- **Pick [AWeber](aff:aweber) Free** (via `aweber.com/free.htm`, not the 14-day paid trial) if you want up to 500 free subscribers with 24/7 phone, email, and chat support, and can check exact branding and automation limits inside the account.
