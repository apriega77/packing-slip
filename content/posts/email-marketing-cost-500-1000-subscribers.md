Many small shops start collecting emails on a $0 plan, then reach two pricing checkpoints during their first year of growth: 500 subscribers and 1,000 subscribers. Below is what [Systeme.io](aff:systeme), [MailerLite](aff:mailerlite), and [AWeber](aff:aweber) charge at both milestones, and what changes on each tier.

## Cost at 500 subscribers

At 500 subscribers, two of the three tools still offer a $0 plan, while MailerLite requires a paid plan because its Free tier locks sending past 250 active subscribers.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Yearly billing | Monthly emails | Key limits at 500 subscribers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Systeme.io Free | $0 | $0 | Unlimited | Up to 2,000 contacts; 1 campaign, 1 rule, 1 workflow, 1 tag |
| Systeme.io Startup | $17.00 / mo | $170.00 / yr | Unlimited | Up to 5,000 contacts; 10 campaigns, 10 rules, 5 workflows, 10 tags |
| MailerLite Comfort | $12.00 / mo | $10.80 / mo ($129.60/yr) | 5,000 | Logo removed, custom domain, 50 automations, 10 sites/pages/forms |
| MailerLite Power | $25.00 / mo | $22.50 / mo ($270.00/yr) | Unlimited (fair use) | Unlimited sites/forms/automations/seats, multi-trigger workflows |
| AWeber Free | $0 | $0 | 3,000 | Up to 500 subscribers; feature caps not tabulated on free page |
| AWeber Lite | $15.00 / mo | $12.50 / mo ($149.99/yr) | 5,000 | 3 automations, 3 landing pages, 1 list, AWeber branding stays |
| AWeber Plus | $30.00 / mo | $20.00 / mo ($239.99/yr) | 6,000 | Branding removed, unlimited automations/pages/lists, split testing |

If you are paying at 500 subscribers, **MailerLite Comfort** is the lowest-cost paid option on both monthly ($12.00/month) and yearly ($129.60/year) billing, and it includes logo removal, custom domains, and 50 automations. **AWeber Free** works at 500 subscribers for $0, though hitting subscriber 501 moves you onto AWeber Lite ($15.00/month or $149.99/year).

## Cost at 1,000 subscribers

Reaching 1,000 subscribers changes the order. AWeber Free ends at 500 subscribers, and MailerLite Comfort steps up to its 1,000-subscriber price bracket, while Systeme.io keeps the same Free and Startup prices because Free covers 2,000 contacts and Startup covers 5,000.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Yearly billing | Monthly emails | Key changes at 1,000 subscribers |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Systeme.io Free | $0 | $0 | Unlimited | Still under 2,000-contact cap; 1 campaign, 1 rule, 1 workflow, 1 tag |
| Systeme.io Startup | $17.00 / mo | $170.00 / yr | Unlimited | Same flat price up to 5,000 contacts |
| MailerLite Comfort | $19.00 / mo | $17.10 / mo ($205.20/yr) | 10,000 | 10x tier email cap; email support |
| MailerLite Power | $39.00 / mo | $35.10 / mo ($421.20/yr) | Unlimited (fair use) | 24/7 live chat and priority email support |
| AWeber Lite | $24.00 / mo | $20.00 / mo ($239.99/yr) | 10,000 | Moves to 501 to 1,000 subscriber tier |
| AWeber Plus | $44.00 / mo | $30.00 / mo ($359.99/yr) | 12,000 | Moves to 501 to 1,000 subscriber tier |

At 1,000 subscribers, **Systeme.io Startup** ($17.00/month monthly or $170.00/year) becomes cheaper than **MailerLite Comfort** ($19.00/month monthly or $205.20/year) and stays at that same price until you cross 5,000 contacts. Note that AWeber also lists a Done For You plan at the same subscription price as Plus with an added setup fee ($79 promotional, crossed out from $599), and displays rounded per-month figures on its annual toggle (such as $12.50/month for $149.99/year).

## Annual totals at a glance

If you pay yearly to keep your shop overhead predictable, total annual spending lines up in this order:

- **At 500 subscribers**: $0 (Systeme.io Free or AWeber Free), then $129.60/year (MailerLite Comfort), $149.99/year (AWeber Lite), $170.00/year (Systeme.io Startup), $239.99/year (AWeber Plus), and $270.00/year (MailerLite Power).
- **At 1,000 subscribers**: $0 (Systeme.io Free), then $170.00/year (Systeme.io Startup), $205.20/year (MailerLite Comfort), $239.99/year (AWeber Lite), $359.99/year (AWeber Plus), and $421.20/year (MailerLite Power).

## Three cost traps to check before upgrading

Price alone does not show what each entry paid tier leaves out:

1. **Branding removal and automation room**: AWeber Lite keeps AWeber branding on emails and landing pages, limits you to 3 email automations, 3 landing pages, and 1 saved custom segment, and leaves out email split testing. Removing branding and lifting those caps on AWeber requires Plus ($239.99/year at 500; $359.99/year at 1,000). MailerLite Comfort includes logo removal, 50 automations, and split testing at its entry paid price. Systeme.io Free limits you to 1 email campaign, 1 automation rule, 1 workflow, and 1 tag until Startup.
2. **Monthly email send limits**: MailerLite Comfort and AWeber Lite both cap sends at 10 times your subscriber tier (5,000 emails/month at 500 subscribers; 10,000/month at 1,000 subscribers). That allows roughly two emails a week to a full list, plus welcome sequences. Systeme.io includes unlimited emails sent on both Free and Startup.
3. **Support channels**: MailerLite Comfort includes email support only (live chat starts on Power or at 50,000+ subscribers). AWeber Lite includes phone, email, and live chat support, and Systeme.io includes 24/7 email support on both Free and Startup.

## Which tier makes sense for your list

- **Stay at $0 with [Systeme.io](aff:systeme) Free** if your shop has up to 2,000 contacts and needs only one welcome automation and one-off newsletters, or move to **Startup** ($17/month or $170/year) once you pass 1,000 contacts and need up to 10 tags and 10 automation rules.
- **Pick [MailerLite](aff:mailerlite) Comfort** ($12/month at 500, $19/month at 1,000) if you want an email-focused workspace without extra funnel and course menus, and want branding removal and multiple automations at the lowest entry paid price at 500 subscribers.
- **Pick [AWeber](aff:aweber)** if you want its 500-subscriber Free plan first (`aweber.com/free.htm`) or value phone and live chat support on Lite ($15/month at 500, $24/month at 1,000) over branding removal and split testing.
